# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience level, identity, or nationality.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and discussions.
- Assume good intent and give concrete, kind feedback.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus on what is best for the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory language.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Spam or other conduct that is clearly inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, or block contributors who violate this policy. To report a concern, contact the maintainer through the email address on their GitHub profile.
